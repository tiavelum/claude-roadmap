Repository tiavelum/claude-roadmap.

The roadmap for working with Claude autonomously and economically; the vision is tiavelum/claude-roadmap#2, and everything else here is issues that serve it.

- Capture an idea as a new issue in tiavelum/claude-roadmap from the idea form, labelled `idea`, whichever repository or conversation it came up in, and do not start work on it.
- Leave it to tiavelum to turn an idea into a building block or close it as not planned.
- Close a `decision` issue only after tiavelum has chosen an option on it, naming the choice in the closing comment.
- Label every step that needs tiavelum `needs-tiavelum`, in whichever repository it lives.
- Change the vision issue only when tiavelum asks for it.
